## Headings and text

Use `##` for sections and `###` for subsections, since the page already has the title as its main heading. Text supports **bold**, *italics*, and `inline code`.

- Keep items short

## Links

Internal links use the site path: [see the Afonse project](/projects/afonse/). External links open in a new tab: [GitHub](https://github.com/PedroHQC).

## Images

Add compressed WebP files to `public/images/blog/<post-slug>/`, then reference them from `/images/...`.

![Afonse exploring the lab](/images/afonse/AfonseLab.webp)

## Videos

Files in the repository stay in its history forever, so prefer YouTube for long or heavy videos with `<YouTube id="VIDEO_ID" title="Short description" />`. For small clips, compress them first and add them to `public/videos/blog/<post-slug>/`:

<Video src="/videos/afonse/AfonseGlideShowCase.mp4" poster="/images/afonse/AfonseWalkingDebug.webp" label="Afonse gliding across a gap" />

Component props must be plain text in quotes: expressions in `{}` are disabled.
